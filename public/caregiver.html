📧 Direct Gmail & Fall Alert Setup

Configure direct delivery so fall warnings land instantly in your personal Gmail inbox (with high-priority sirens and red emergency banners).

⚡ How to connect your Gmail (Takes 30 seconds):

  1. Go to Google Account → App Passwords ↗
  2. Make sure 2-Step Verification is active on your Google Account.
  3. Under App name, type ElderCare and click Create.
  4. Copy the 16-letter code generated by Google (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop) and paste it below.
