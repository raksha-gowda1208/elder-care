ElderCare Vision

Intelligent fall detection & spoken medication assistant for elders living alone

Stay connected & protected, even from miles away.

ElderCare connects caregivers with elderly family members living alone. The home device runs real-time skeleton pose fall detection, plays scheduled voice medication alarms with automatic microphone listening for “taken / done”, and sends immediate emergency emails upon fall detection.
